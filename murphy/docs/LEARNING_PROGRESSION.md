# Learning progression

This is the manual record of course coverage, maintained under the authoritative
[teaching standards](TEACHING_STANDARDS.md). Consult it before preparing a lesson
and update it incrementally through the existing
[production workflow](PRODUCTION_WORKFLOW.md). No schema fields or automation are
required.

## Recording rules

- Add a unit only as its lesson is developed and reviewed. Do not prepopulate the
  curriculum, infer teaching from a unit title or invent future unit content.
- Keep **Planned teaching** separate from **Approved teaching**. Proposals and
  script-only coverage remain planned, even when the script has been approved.
  They are not evidence of a prerequisite already taught.
- At the existing human review of the lesson's teaching content, confirm what is
  actually taught in the reviewed version and add it to Approved teaching. For
  silent versions, this means teaching present in the visuals, not intended future
  narration. Reuse that review; do not add an approval stage.
- Link the episode source/version and existing approval notes, including review
  date, so coverage can be checked. Record only concepts and term explanations
  present in that version. Approval here establishes reviewed coverage, not
  publication, narrated production readiness or learner mastery.
- Use short phrases. For prerequisites and connections, cite earlier approved
  unit entries and describe the relevant knowledge or relationship. Use `None`
  or `Not yet verified` explicitly where appropriate. Keep a deferred concept's
  reason; specify a destination unit only if verified.
- When coverage changes, revise the planning notes and reconcile the approved
  entry at the existing review of the revised lesson. Preserve the version and
  approval reference for earlier coverage if it differs. Do not silently treat a
  new draft as approved or count infrastructure fixtures as curriculum teaching.

## Entry format

Use the same small template in the appropriate section below. Move confirmed
items into Approved teaching; retain any untaught proposals in Planned teaching.
This template is a format example, not a curriculum entry.

```markdown
### Unit <number>

- Episode/version and source: <link to the lesson version being described>
- Review: <planning/script status, or existing approval note link and date>
- New concepts introduced: <concepts and scope>
- Terminology explained: <terms defined with examples>
- Prerequisite knowledge: <earlier approved units and required concepts>
- Connections to previous lessons: <how earlier knowledge is used or extended>
- Concepts intentionally deferred: <concept and reason; destination only if verified>
```
