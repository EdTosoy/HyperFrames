# Unit 6 asset provenance

Context icons: Lucide Contributors, pinned release 0.468.0. [Official license](https://lucide.dev/license): ISC, with Feather-derived portions under MIT. Source bytes and all notices are preserved in `provider/`; no icon dependency or runtime download. Reuse, modification and redistribution are permitted with notices retained.

Original 720×320 instructional arrangements combine those icons with labels, explicit negation crosses and precisely aligned two-week calendars. Custom calendar geometry is needed to show exact absence versus occasional events. The records illustrate declared habits; no numerical frequency definition is implied. Crosses mean the labeled activity or preference is absent, not that an object cannot exist. No textbook material was copied or traced.

The shared Manrope and GSAP assets retain their existing manifest and notices.
