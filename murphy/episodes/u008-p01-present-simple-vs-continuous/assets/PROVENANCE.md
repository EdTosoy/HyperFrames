Lucide icons are pinned to 0.468.0. [Official license](https://lucide.dev/license): ISC and applicable Feather MIT. Unchanged provider SVG bytes and both notices are kept in `provider/`; composites embed notices and document assets carry notices into generated projects. Downloaded only the selected water, heart, knowledge, hospital, television and phone icons; other needed icons reuse the preserved Units 6–7 provider bytes. No new dependency or runtime request.

Original Unit 3 kitchen/spoon, desk/pen and court/ball geometry is copied unchanged under Unit 8 IDs. Original Unit 5 bird geometry is also copied unchanged. Those earlier source files remain intact. Custom arrangements/calendars preserve the same people and objects across now and routine views. Calendar counts illustrate declared routines, not numerical definitions of frequency words. No textbook content was copied or traced.
