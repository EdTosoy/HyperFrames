# Unit 7 asset provenance

Original instructional arrangements use pinned Lucide 0.468.0 icons. [Official license](https://lucide.dev/license): ISC and applicable Feather-derived MIT. The source SVG bytes and both permission notices are retained in `provider/`; composites embed notices, and document assets carry license files into generated projects. No package or runtime network dependency was added.

New context icons were downloaded from pinned upstream source. User/users, book, bicycle, train and car source bytes were reused unchanged from Unit 6. Provider notices identify Lucide Contributors and Cole Bemis. Reuse, modification and redistribution are permitted with notices retained.

Original diagrams are necessary for exact grammar animation: separate positive endings move into a removed tray while the complete base form is restored. The ending letters are not mechanically added to do to spell does. The study and have transformations explicitly restore the whole base word. Calendar cells/cursor show a declared routine, not a fixed percentage definition. All arrangements, labels, questions and exercises are original; no textbook material was copied or traced.

Shared Manrope and GSAP retain their existing manifest and notices.
